# Sources - relevant literature and searching for literature

Via the (digital) [library from The Hague University of Applied Sciences](https://www.dehaagsehogeschool.nl/over-de-haagse/faciliteiten/bibliotheek), you have access to many online sources. A nice searchable overview is given in this rather textual [ezproxy page](https://login.ezproxy.hhs.nl/menu) (Ezproxy is a service to access when you are not on the HHS network, e.g. to access [Springer Link](http://ezproxy.hhs.nl/login?url=https://link.springer.com) or [IEEE Explore](http://ezproxy.hhs.nl/login?url=https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/).):
- [Libkey Nomad](https://thirdiron.com/downloadnomad/) - Essential browser plugin for getting easy access to literature via The Hague University of Applied Sciences (must have for every researcher)
- [Libkey](https://libkey.io) - Get quick access through [DOI code](https://www.doi.org/) of paper/article.
- [Browzine](https://browzine.com/) - Browse / find complete journals on topics (login with HHS account)
- [Learning Oreilly](https://learning.oreilly.com/) - Login with your HHS username and password and get access to many good books in the field of coding and computer sciences.
- [NEN Connect](https://connect.nen.nl/Account/SSO/SURFconext) - Access to many NEN/ISO norms (login via this link, via ezproxy does not work!)
- [Web of Science](https://www-webofscience-com.ezproxy.hhs.nl/wos/woscc/smart-search) - Search tool for reliable scientific literature search ([Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com) is useful, quick and easy but not always reliable in finding quality scientific literature.)
- [Springer Link](https://link-springer-com.ezproxy.hhs.nl/) - Get access to many sources, papers, handbooks, etc. from Springer publisher.
- [IEEE Explore](https://ieeexplore-ieee-org.ezproxy.hhs.nl/Xplore/home.jsp) - Search and get the extensive amount papers from the engineering organisation IEEE
- [ScienceDirect](https://www-sciencedirect-com.ezproxy.hhs.nl/) - Search and get the extensive amount papers from Elsevier journals
- [ACM Digital Library](https://dl-acm-org.ezproxy.hhs.nl/) - Journals from Association for Computing Machinery (open access)
- [ASME (America Association of Mechanical Engineers)](https://asmedigitalcollection.asme.org/journals) - Many journals are also open access or can be accessed through HHS/Nomad browser plugin
- [EBSCOhost](http://ezproxy.hhs.nl/login?url=https://search.ebscohost.com/login.aspx) - access to many books/publications, not very user friendly but sometimes a last resort wheb all other do not work
- [HBO Kennisbank](https://hbo-kennisbank.nl/index) - Search for open thesis and reports from universities of applied sciences in the Netherlands
- [Publinova](https://publinova.nl/en) - Search for projects / results / teams from universities of applied sciences in the Netherlands
- [APA guides](https://auteursrechten.nl/en/citing-sources/apa-guidelines/) - Guide on how to cite
- [RefWorks](https://refworks.proquest.com/) - Useful tool for dealing with references in Word documents (tool not accessible yet, but is in progress via HHS Tool Route)
- [Navigating the Scholarly Literature: A Practical Guide to Searching Effectively (Without Too Much Stress)](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpz1.70138) - article on searching scientific sources by Kathryn P. Kohn
